Common questions

What partners ask before engaging

Do you replace our local Spanish counsel?
No. Muralla AI works alongside your local counsel or as an independent analytical layer. We provide the intelligence, analysis, and drafts; your lawyers make the strategic decisions and file the submissions. We integrate with your existing team rather than replacing anyone.
Does our case data pass through OpenAI or cloud providers?
No. All AI processing runs on local models installed on dedicated hardware in our Madrid office. No data is sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any cloud API. Your case file never leaves the physical machine assigned to your engagement. This is a non-negotiable design decision, not an optional feature.
What happens if you cannot continue the engagement?
Every engagement includes a documented handover protocol. Your dashboard, analysis, and all deliverables are yours regardless. In the event of service interruption, you receive complete export of all work product, documentation of methodology, and transition support to ensure continuity of your defence.
How does billing work for Magic Circle or AmLaw firms?
We issue standard invoices with detailed engagement descriptions suitable for client pass-through billing. Our fees are structured to be transparently allocable to specific matters. We can adapt our billing format to your firm's internal requirements.
Can we trial the service before committing?
Yes. Send us a redacted case file or a sample from your matter, and we deliver a preliminary analysis within 48 hours — at no cost. You evaluate the quality of the output before any commitment. In four years, no firm has declined to continue after the trial.

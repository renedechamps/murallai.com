Decision tree: exit scenarios

Click on each scenario for details. Probabilities reflect Muralla AI's assessment based on comparable case law and the current state of the proceedings.

A. Sobreseimiento (dismissal) at cuestiones previas 20%
The Sala upholds prescripción for offense B) and ne bis in idem for offense A) at the cuestiones previas stage.
Outcome: Sobreseimiento libre (full dismissal). Navarro is absuelto (acquitted) of all charges before juicio oral. Pena: 0. Fianza (bail) refund.

Strategy: Requires a flawless escrito de cuestiones previas and a Sala willing to rule before juicio oral. Low probability but maximum impact. Minimal procedural cost.
Key case law: STS 467/2018 (autonomous prescripción); STC 177/1999 (ne bis in idem).
B. Absolución (acquittal) after juicio oral 35%
Juicio oral takes place. The Sala finds error de tipo and insuficiencia de la acusación proven.
Outcome: Sentencia absolutoria (not guilty verdict). Pena: 0. Possible compensation for wrongful prisión provisional (pretrial detention).

Strategy: Primary line: error de tipo (art. 14.1 CP). Mr. Navarro believed he was executing legitimate transactions. Support: the CEO's emails demonstrate that instructions did not reveal the tax scheme. Forensic IT expert report (pericial informática) proves actual use of the software.
Risk: The Sala may find that a CFO "should have known" of the irregularity (imprudencia grave / gross negligence).
C. Conformidad (negotiated plea deal) with minimum penas 25%
The Fiscalía (prosecution) offers a conformidad with minimum penas after assessing the weakness of its case against Navarro.
Outcome: Pena suspendida (suspended sentence) + multa reducida (reduced fine). Estimate: 6-12 months prisión (suspended) + EUR 50,000 multa. No ingreso en prisión (no imprisonment).

Strategy: Procedural attrition through well-founded weekly escritos ("adapted persistent pressure"). Each escrito raises the cost of maintaining the acusación (prosecution). The CEO's conformidad creates an inescapable proportionality precedent: if the autor intelectual (mastermind) received 2 years suspended, pursuing 7 for the ejecutor material (material executor) is unsustainable.
Conditions for acceptance: Only if it includes suspensión de la pena + reduced pena under 2 years + no antecedentes penales (criminal record).
This is the scenario recommended by Muralla AI as optimal in cost/benefit terms.
D. Condena (conviction) with significant atenuantes (mitigating factors) 15%
Condena for offense A) with atenuantes of dilaciones indebidas and analogous cooperation.
Outcome: Pena reducida (reduced sentence). Estimate: 2-3 years prisión (possible suspensión) + EUR 500,000 multa. Viable recurso de casación (appeal to the Tribunal Supremo).

Atenuantes available: Dilaciones indebidas (4 years of instrucción), effective cooperation (Navarro has provided exhaustive testimony), partial reparación del daño (if he deposits a sum).
Post-sentencing strategy: Recurso de casación focusing on the application of error de tipo and proportionality with the CEO's pena.
E. Condena close to the pena solicitada (requested sentence) 5%
Worst-case scenario: the Sala convicts on both offenses without significant atenuantes.
Outcome: Pena severa (severe sentence). Estimate: 5-7 years prisión + full multa. Ingreso en prisión.

Why this is unlikely (5%): The CEO's conformidad at 2 years suspended makes it virtually impossible for the Sala to impose 7 on the ejecutor material. The prescripción for offense B) is solid. The pericial de la defensa (defense expert report) proves the services were real.
Contingency strategy: Recurso de casación + recurso de amparo (constitutional appeal to the TC) for disproportionality (art. 14 CE). ECHR as last resort.

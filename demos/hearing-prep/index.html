Guía de preparación

Antes de la vista oral

  • Review the 2018–2022 transaction timeline with the client
  • Prepare short, direct answers for all 12 questions
  • Practice emotional control when facing provocative questions
  • Review accounting documentation submitted as prueba documental
  • Confirm technical invoicing points with the perito (expert witness)

During the interrogatorio

  • Keep answers brief: yes/no plus one explanatory sentence at most
  • Do not guess exact figures — “I don’t recall precisely”
  • Never contradict statements given during the fase de instrucción (investigation phase)
  • Avoid making legal or technical accounting assessments
  • Look at the Sala, not the Fiscal, when answering

Frases de seguridad

  • “Me acojo a mi derecho a no contestar sobre ese punto”
  • “Eso excede mis funciones como Director Financiero”
  • “Necesitaría consultar la documentación para ser preciso”
  • “Esa cuestión ya fue abordada en mi declaración en instrucción”

Señales de alerta

  • Fiscal repeats the same question with different wording
  • Questions about personal relationships with the CEO
  • References to documents not included in the sumario
  • Fiscal cites coacusado statements out of context
